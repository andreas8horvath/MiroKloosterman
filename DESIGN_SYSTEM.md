# Brand Book, Design System & Moodboard Specification
## Miro Kloosterman — Healer, Coach, & Retreat Guide

This document serves as the definitive creative blueprint and absolute source of truth for the Miro Kloosterman brand. It contains everything required for a branding agency, UI designer, editorial photographer, or creative director to design marketing assets, curate physical spaces, film content, and construct moodboards that align perfectly with Miro's premium somatic practices.

---

# 1. Brand Strategy & Identity Core

Miro Kloosterman’s practice resides at the rare intersection of **rigorous physical therapy, deep subconscious hypnotherapy, and sacred Balinese somatic water ceremonies**. The brand identity must reject the clichés of both sterile medical clinics and overly saturated, dogmatic spiritual movements. Instead, it speaks in the language of **quiet luxury, raw nature, grounded realism, and restorative safety**.

### Brand Pillars
* **Somatic Integration:** The belief that the physical skeleton, emotional nervous system, and subconscious mind form a single, unbroken grid.
* **Sacred Safety:** Cultivating a clinical-grade sense of emotional and physical safety, allowing clients to fully surrender tension and trauma.
* **Grounded Spiritual Realism:** Practical, down-to-earth support tailored to high-performing professionals, combining physical anatomy with energetic restoration.

### Tone of Voice
* **Direct Yet Gentle:** Speaks clearly and honestly without hiding behind spiritual jargon. 
* **Quietly Confident:** Avoids over-promising, hype-marketing, or self-aggrandizing language. The work speaks through felt experiences.
* **Warm and Rooted:** Grounded in nature, human connection, and physical realism.

# 3. Photography & Art Direction

Photography for Miro Kloosterman must feel completely authentic, editorial, and documentary-grade. Avoid stock photos with flat, artificial studio lighting.

```
       LIGHTING Direction              COMPOSITION Rule             TEXTURE Standard
   +-------------------------+    +-------------------------+    +--------------------+
   |  6500K Morning Glow     |    |  Asymmetrical Balance   |    |  Natural Film Grain|
   |           to            |    |  Generous Negative Space|    |  Soft Motion Blur  |
   |  2700K Sunset Amber     |    |  The Human Detail       |    |  Avoid Flat Digital|
   +-------------------------+    +-------------------------+    +--------------------+
```

### 1. Lighting & Atmosphere
* **The "Light-Bloom" Principle:** Light should always appear natural and directional—never artificial. Prioritize golden hour morning mist (6500K shifting to soft amber) and late-afternoon sunset flares (2700K).
* **High-Contrast Shadows:** Emphasize soft shadow play, such as tropical leaves casting organic silhouettes onto linen walls.

### 2. Subject & Composition
* **Candid Somatic Interaction:** When capturing Miro during sessions (breathwork, body reset, water therapy), focus on unposed, high-honesty documentary shots. Capture a steady hand holding a client's head in water, the rise of a chest on a breathwork mat, or a calm, focused gaze.
* **The "Human Detail":** Crop closely on expressive, quiet details—warm hands resting on volcanic stones, water droplets falling from a fingertips, or bare feet resting on warm wooden floors.
* **Scale and Negative Space:** Place subjects offset in the frame, surrounded by generous natural environments (dense green jungle, soft sand, stone temple walls) to evoke a feeling of spaciousness.

### 3. Color Grading & Texture
* **Desaturated Jungle Greens:** Pull back the yellow-saturation in green foliage to prevent neon-bright "travel influencer" aesthetics. Greens should look deep, rich, and forest-like.
* **Warm Organic Skin-Tones:** Keep skin-tones healthy, warm, and natural.
* **Analog Feel:** Introduce a very subtle, fine-grained texture to all brand photography to enhance tactile, physical intimacy and reduce sterile digital sharpness.

# 5. Typography & Editorial Hierarchy

We pair a high-fashion, classical serif with a modern, clean geometric sans-serif to establish visual balance and rhythm.

### 1. Typography Selection

#### Display Typeface: **Cormorant Garamond**
* **Style:** Serif
* **Google Font Import:** `Cormorant_Garamond`
* **Tailwind Token:** `font-display`
* **Aesthetic Role:** Classical elegance, high-fashion editorial, poetic confidence.
* **Character Styling:** Always style with light weights (`font-light`), high line-heights (`leading-normal` or `leading-relaxed`), and spacious letter-spacing (`tracking-wide` or `tracking-wider`).

#### Body Typeface: **Outfit**
* **Style:** Sans-Serif
* **Google Font Import:** `Outfit`
* **Tailwind Token:** `font-body`
* **Aesthetic Role:** Structural clarity, clean spacing, geometric readability.
* **Character Styling:** Style with regular or light weights (`font-light` or `font-normal`) and generous leading (`leading-relaxed`) to prevent user cognitive fatigue during long prose.

# 6. Geometric & Architectural Layout Rules

The structural grid is based on Balinese temple architecture: balance, thresholds, and transition.

```
       [ SACRED ARCH ]              [ ASYMMETRICAL SPLIT ]         [ WHITE-SPACE BUFFER ]
     .-----------------.            +--------------------+         +--------------------+
    /                   \           |   60% Narrative    |         |                    |
   |                     |          |   Editorial Text   |         |     Clean Space    |
   |                     |          |                    |         |                    |
   |     Image Frame     |          +--------------------+         |  [ Content Card ]  |
   |                     |          |   40% Visual Arch  |         |                    |
   |                     |          |   Balanced Relief  |         |     Clean Space    |
   '---------------------'          +--------------------+         +--------------------+
```

### 1. The Sacred Temple Arch (`.arch-mask`)
* **Visual Representation:** Mimics the split gates (*Candi Bentar*) and sacred thresholds of Balinese sanctuaries, guiding the visitor from outer chaos into inner quiet.
* **CSS Specification:** 
  ```css
  .arch-mask {
    border-top-left-radius: 9999px;
    border-top-right-radius: 9999px;
    border-bottom-left-radius: 0px;
    border-bottom-right-radius: 0px;
  }
  ```
* **Application:** Used exclusively on structural portraits of Miro, retreat sanctuaries, and water therapy hero images to elevate standard rectangle images into architectural highlights.

### 2. Asymmetric Column Alignment
* Avoid rigid, clinical grid alignments. Layouts should utilize an alternating, asymmetrical rhythm.
* **Odd/Even Layout Cadence:** 
  * On *Even* cards, place textual narratives on the left and framed arches on the right.
  * On *Odd* cards, reverse the order (framed arches on the left, textual narratives on the right).
  * Align narratives with slight offsets (e.g., `lg:col-span-6` paired with `lg:col-span-5` and a `1-column offset gap`) to introduce architectural elegance and visual breathing room.

### 3. Spacing Rhythm & Margins
* Never let content touch the viewport edge. Maintain a minimum margin of `px-6` on mobile devices and `px-12` or `max-w-7xl mx-auto` on desktop displays.
* Ensure generous margins between page sections (`py-20` on mobile scaling to `py-28 md:py-36` on desktop) to allow the user’s mind to rest and integrate information while scrolling.

# 7. Kinetic & Interaction Guidelines

Animations must serve a clear purpose—guiding focus, slowing down the user’s breathing, and providing soothing feedback. Never use hyperactive, rapid, or jarring transitions.

### 1. Scroll-Reveal Configurations
All elements should float gently onto the screen using custom cubic-bezier curves mimicking wind.
* **Animation Type:** Fluid Fade-and-Glide (`ScrollReveal`).
* **Directional Cues:**
  * **Upward entry:** Standard for general headings and cards.
  * **Asymmetrical sliding (Left/Right):** Used for narratives to slide towards their corresponding images.
* **Framer Motion Constants:**
  * **Standard Transition:** `duration: 0.85`, `ease: [0.16, 1, 0.3, 1]` (custom cubic-bezier curve for soft deceleration).
  * **Staggered Delay:** Elements in a list must be delayed sequentially: `delay: index * 0.05`.

### 2. The Interactive Breathing Guide Component

A signature interactive element on the Biography page designed to help clients synchronize their breath.

```
       [ INHALE Phase ]                 [ HOLD Phase ]                 [ EXHALE Phase ]
     O o .                 . o O     o o .                 . o o     . . .                 . . .
   o       o             o       o  o     o               o     o   .     .               .     .
  o  (Pulse) o   ===>   o  (Max)  o o      o (Glow Bloom) o      o .       .   ===>      .       .
   o       o             o       o  o     o               o     o   .     .               .     .
     O o .                 . o O     o o .                 . o o     . . .                 . . .
    Scale: 1.05 -> 1.35             Scale: Steady 1.35              Scale: 1.35 -> 1.00
    Duration: 4 seconds             Duration: 4 seconds             Duration: 4 seconds
```

* **Core Purpose:** Mimics diaphragmatic expansion and contraction, creating an immediate, physical sensation of calm.
* **Interactive Behavior:**
  * **Pulse Sphere Background:** Scales outward from `1.0` to `1.3` during inhalation, glows softly, and scales inward back to `1.0` during exhalation.
  * **Phases:** Loop consists of **Inhale** (4 seconds) $\rightarrow$ **Hold** (4 seconds) $\rightarrow$ **Exhale** (4 seconds) $\rightarrow$ **Hold** (4 seconds).
* **Kinetic Parameters:**
  * **Scale Spring:** `type: "tween"`, `ease: "easeInOut"`, `duration: 4.0`.
  * **Inner Core Text:** Displays clear, calming text ("Inhale", "Hold", "Exhale") using the geometric `font-body text-[10px]` in Grounding Rose-Clay with a `tracking-widest` uppercase styling.

### 3. Interactive Buttons & Form Fields
* **Primary Interactive Link Hover:** Text elements colored with `#C08264` (Rose-Clay) must shift to deep sunset wood `#8C5135` upon hover. Transition should use a slow, calming speed: `duration-300 ease-in-out`.
* **Form Inputs:** Borders must use `#F2ECE3` by default, expanding to `#C08264` upon active focus. All focus changes must transition smoothly with `transition-colors duration-300`.

# 8. Brand Guardianship & Anti-Patterns

To maintain the high-fashion, premium, and human-centric tone of the brand, designers must adhere to these strict exclusions:

* **No "AI-Slop" or Tech-Larping Elements:** Under no circumstances should you add network status lights, port indicators, system coordinates, latency pings, or simulated console logs (e.g., no `● ONLINE` indicators, no `PORT: 3000` text, and no mock debug lines).
* **No Default Gradients:** Strictly avoid generic, high-saturation purple-to-blue or blue-to-pink digital gradients. Gradients are permitted only as soft radial backdrops (light-blooms) mimicking natural sunlight in warm Terracotta (`#D9A38A` at `10% opacity`).
* **No Cluttered Interfaces:** If a section contains fewer than three sentences, do not decorate it with floating shapes, thin borders, or decorative lines. Let the beautiful negative space of the warm linen canvas stand entirely alone.
* **No Hyperactive Transitions:** Avoid fast-spinning loading icons, bouncing buttons, or sharp, jarring animations. Every interactive change must feel heavy, deliberate, and smooth.
